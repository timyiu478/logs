#### Sliding Window: LC-2024. Maximize the Confusion of an Exam

Key Intuition:

1. Problem Reduction: The problem asks for the longest contiguous window of either 'T's or 'F's given at most k changes. We can break this into two symmetric sub-problems:
   - (1) Find the max consecutive 'T's by flipping 'F's.
   - (2) Find the max consecutive 'F's by flipping 'T's.
   - Return the maximum of both results.

2. Contiguous Window Constraint: To maximize consecutive elements, our operations must form a contiguous subarray. 

Implementation Strategy (Queue-Based Sliding Window):

- Use a queue to store the indices of characters that differed from our target (i.e., the ones we "spent an operation" to change).
- When our budget k drops below 0, pop the oldest modified index from the queue and advance the left boundary `i = q.popleft() + 1` to restore the budget.

Key Takeaways:

- Translate qualitative operations ("change a character") into quantitative constraints ("count the mismatches")
    - Cost function: $M(i, j)$ be the number of characters in that window that are not equal to 'T'.
        - Property: monotonically non-decreasing

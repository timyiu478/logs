#### Greedy: LC-2333. Minimum Sum of Squared Difference

* Why one operation of k should use the index that has the current maximum difference?
    * $x^2 - (x-1)^2 = 2x - 1$
    * Because 2x−1 strictly increases with x, reducing a larger difference always yields a greater decrease in the total sum of squared differences
* Binary Search Approach:
    * Search Space: The absolute differences
    * Guess: A target maximum difference

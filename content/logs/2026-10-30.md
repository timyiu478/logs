#### Graph: LC-3970. Shortest Path With At Most K Consecutive Identical Characters

Intuition:

* **State Definition:** Use a variant of Dijkstra's algorithm tracking the minimum cost for each `(node, consecutive_count)` state rather than just the node.
* **Pruning:** Discard paths where `consecutive_count > k` or if a cheaper/equal cost to the same `(node, consecutive_count)` state already exists.
* Why `consecutive_count`? It encodes the path history needed to enforce the problem constraint. It tells us whether the next move is legal or not.

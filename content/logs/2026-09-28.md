#### HashMap: LC-2260. Minimum Consecutive Cards to Pick Up

Key Insight: The minimum number of consecutive cards containing a match is equal to the minimum index difference between two identical cards + 1.

#### Greedy: LC-2957. Remove Adjacent Almost-Equal Characters

Key Insight: 

* When we encounter two adjacent characters word[i-1] and word[i] that are almost-equal, changing word[i] is always optimal compared to changing word[i-1]. Changing word[i] breaks the conflict with word[i-1] and gives us a free pass for word[i+1].
* Since we can pick any letter out of 26, we can easily choose one that is not almost-equal to either word[i-1] or word[i+1].

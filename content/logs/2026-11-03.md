#### Greedy: LC-2139. Minimum Moves to Reach Target Score

**Intuitions:**

* **DP Bottleneck:** DP with state `(current_score, used_doubles)` leads to TLE because the target can be up to $10^9$.
* **Inversion (Working Backwards):** Go from `target` down to `1`.
    * *Why:* Eliminates the guesswork of when to double. Dividing a larger number preserves maximum benefit.
* **Optimization:** When `maxDoubles` runs out, the remaining moves to reach `1` are simply `current_score - 1` ($O(1)$ time).

#### Monotonic Problem: LC-768. Max Chunks To Make Sorted II

1. The stack structure: A monotonic stack maintains the maximum value (peak) of each active chunk in a strictly increasing order.
2. The merge rule: When an incoming element is smaller than the stack's top (`n < s[-1]`), it violates the monotonic invariant, forcing us to merge previous chunks by popping and tracking the running maximum (`m = max(m, s.pop())`).

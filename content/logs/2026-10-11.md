#### Math: LC-2033. Minimum Operations to Make a Uni-Value Grid

* To transform any two numbers, $a$ and $b$, into the exact same target value $v$ using only additions and subtractions of $x$, they must share the same remainder when divided by $x$.
    * $v = a + k_a \cdot x \quad \text{and} \quad v = b + k_b \cdot x$
    * $v - a = k_a \cdot x$
    * $v \pmod x = a \pmod x$

* The median of the numbers is a value we make all numbers equal to

#### Math: LC-1551. Minimum Operations to Make Array Equal

* Array: arr[i] = 2 * i + 1
* Key Insight: The target value (median) of the array is always $n$.
* Logic: Total operations = sum of distances from elements in the lower half to the target $n$.

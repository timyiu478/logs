#### Sliding Window: LC-2461. Maximum Sum of Distinct Subarrays With Length K

Core Logic:

* Maintain a sliding window of size $K$ while tracking the running sum (`total`) and element frequencies (`ht`).
* Add the incoming element `nums[i]` and remove the outgoing element `nums[i - k]` at each step to maintain an $O(1)$ sum update.
* Delete keys from the hash map when their frequency reaches `0`. This guarantees that `len(ht) == k` serves as an $O(1)$ check to confirm all elements in the current window are unique.

#### Array: LC-3346. Maximum Frequency of an Element After Performing Operations I

Key Idea:

1. Use hashmap freq to record the frequency of numbers in nums.
2. Use a difference array to track how many elements can reach each target in
   [min(nums) - k, max(nums) + k].
     * We don't care about numOperations yet in this array.
3. Sweep through all possible target values from min_target to max_target:
     * current_f is the total number of elements that can reach the target.
     * Number of successfully modified values: min(current_f - freq[target],
       numOperations).
     * Total frequency at target = freq[target] + min(current_f - freq[target],
       numOperations).

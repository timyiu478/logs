#### Dynamic Programming: LC-464. Can I Win

Edge Cases:

* sum(1..maxChoosableInteger) < desiredTotal -> the running total never can reach to the desiredTotal

Key Insight:

* Player 1 win if running total >= desiredTotal or player 2 cannot force win with state (running total + n, mask | 1 << n)
* The current player wins if:
    * Their move causes the running total to $\ge$ desiredTotal.
    * OR they can make a move that leads to a state where the opponent cannot force a win.
* why a problem exhibits overlapping subproblems:
    * the bitmask captured the exact set of remaining choices regardless of the order they were picked in.

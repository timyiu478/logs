#### Stack: LC-224. Basic Calculator

Insights:
    
* Operators belong to the following number, not the preceding one.
    * How? Maintain a `sign` variable (+1 or -1) buffering the last seen operator. When a number finishes, apply `res += sign * num`.
    * Flush Triggers: Numbers finish on operators (`+`/`-`), closing parens `)`, or End-of-String.
* Nesting forces Last-Suspended, First-Resumed execution:
    * `(`: Freeze current state (`res` and `sign`), push to stack, and **reset local scope** (`res = 0`, `sign = 1`).
    * `)`: Pop `parent_sign` and `parent_res`, then merge: `res = parent_res + (parent_sign * inner_res)`.

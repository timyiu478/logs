#### Binary Search: LC-3508. Implement Router

Key Takeaways:

* Global Monotonic Stream: Packets arrive with non-decreasing timestamps, ensuring natural time ordering across all destinations.
* Localized Eviction: Because arrivals are monotonic globally, each destination's active timestamps are also monotonic. Evictions/forwards strictly hit the leftmost element.
* Range Queries: Purging evicted packets from the front leaves a clean, sorted collection of active packets, making getCount a direct binary search (bisect_right - bisect_left).

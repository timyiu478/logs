#### Monotonic Deque: LC-239. Sliding Window Maximum

Intuitions:

* I have solved LC-1696. Jump Game VI which one of its subproblem is finding the sliding window maximum.
    * Pattern: Monotonic Deque maintaining candidate indices in both temporal (window bounds) and value (descending order) dimensions.

Loop Invariants:

* i-k < index in the deque <= i
* nums[deque[j]] < nums[deque[k]] for all j > k

#### Sliding Window: LC-1838. Frequency of the Most Frequent Element

Key Intuition:

1. Target Selection: To maximize the frequency of any element A, the most efficient strategy is to take elements already present in the array that are smaller than A and increment them up to A.
2. Contiguous Subarray: After sorting the array, the elements closest to a target nums[j] will always form a contiguous subarray nums[i...j] ending at j.

Key Algorithm:

1. Sort nums so that we can evaluate all potential targets efficiently in O(N) using a sliding window.
2. Sliding Window Expansion: 
   - When expanding the right boundary to j (making nums[j] the target), the cost to lift all previous elements in the window increases by: 
     (nums[j] - nums[j-1]) * (j - i)
3. Sliding Window Shrinking: 
   - If the total cost exceeds k, increment the left boundary i and subtract (nums[j] - nums[i]) from the total cost until it fits within budget k.
4. Track the maximum window size (j - i + 1) throughout the sweep.

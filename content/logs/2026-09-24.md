#### Sliding Window: LC-1423. Maximum Points You Can Obtain from Cards

* **Core Idea:** Maximizing the score of k cards taken from either end is equivalent to minimizing the sum of the remaining fixed-size middle window (size = N - k).
* **Approach:** Use a fixed-size sliding window of length N - k. Slide it across the array to find the minimum middle sum, then subtract that from the total array sum.

#### Greedy: LC-3723. Maximize Sum of Squares of Digits

* When a solution is impossible: If 9 * num < sum, no valid integer can be formed because the absolute maximum possible sum of digits for a length of num is $9 \times \text{num}$.
* Greedy Rule: Greedily pick the largest possible digit ($9$) at each step as long as the remaining sum can support it.

```python
while num > 0 or sum > 0:
    if sum >= 9:
        arr.append("9")
        num -= 1
        sum -= 9
    else:
        arr.append(str(sum))
        num -= 1
        sum = 0
```

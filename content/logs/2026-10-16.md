#### Interval: LC-57. Insert Interval

3 Phase:

1. Before: An existing interval ends before the new interval even begins.
    * Condition: `intervals[i][1] < newInterval[0]`
2. Collision: Intervals that physically crash into or overlap with your new interval
    * Condition: `intervals[i][0] <= newInterval[1]`
3. After: Everything else left in the array

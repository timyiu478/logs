#### Math: LC-2929. Distribute Candies Among Children II

##### Key Takeaways

* **Inclusion-Exclusion Principle:**  $\text{Valid Ways} = \text{Total Unrestricted Ways} - (\text{Ways with } \ge 1 \text{ child exceeding limit}) + (\text{Ways with } \ge 2 \text{ children exceeding limit}) - (\text{Ways with } 3 \text{ children exceeding limit})$

* **Stars and Bars Method:**  The total number of ways to distribute $k$ non-negative candies among $3$ children with **no upper bound** is given by: $$\binom{k + 3 - 1}{3 - 1} = \binom{k + 2}{2} = \frac{(k + 2)(k + 1)}{2} \quad (\text{for } k \ge 0)$$

* **Handling Upper Bounds ($> \text{limit}$):**  To find the number of distributions where $i$ specific children receive more than `limit` candies, pre-assign $(\text{limit} + 1)$ candies to each of those $i$ children first. The remaining $k = n - i \cdot (\text{limit} + 1)$ candies can then be freely distributed among all 3 children using Stars and Bars.

* **Edge Cases:**  If $n - i \cdot (\text{limit} + 1) < 0$, it is impossible for $i$ children to exceed the limit simultaneously, so that term contributes $0$ ways.

#### Inversion, Sliding Window: 992. Subarrays with K Different Integers

Intuitions:

* Number of subarrays with k different integers = number of subarrays with at most k different integers - number of subarrays with at most k-1 different integers.
* We can use sliding window approach to find number of subarrays with at most k different integers in O(n) time.
    * For each fixed index r, there are r-l+1 number of valid subarrays that ending at r where l is the leftmost index such that there are at most k different integers in nums[l:r].
    * Why sliding window works: distinct(nums[i..j]) is monotone
        * expanding r: only keep or increase distinct(nums[i..j])
        * shrinking l: only keep or decrease distinct(nums[i..j])

Loop Invariants:

* l <= r
* l is the smallest valid left endpoint for fixed r
* distinct(nums[l:r]) <= k
